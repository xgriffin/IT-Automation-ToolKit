# Laptop won't turn on or charge

## Symptoms
- Laptop won't power on, black screen
- Battery or charging light doesn't come on
- Laptop only works while plugged in

## Resolution
1. Try a different wall outlet and confirm the charger's light is on.
2. Check that the charger cable is fully seated at both ends. If you use a dock, plug the charger straight into the laptop.
3. Hard reset: unplug the charger, hold the power button for 30 seconds, then plug in and power on.
4. Leave it charging for 30 minutes, then try again.
5. If there's still no power, IT swaps the charger. If that doesn't fix it, IT issues a loaner and opens a warranty repair.
