# VPN won't connect from home

## Symptoms
- VPN client says "connection failed" or times out
- Connected, but can't reach internal resources

## Resolution
1. Confirm home internet works (open any public website).
2. Fully quit and reopen the VPN client, then reconnect.
3. Approve the MFA prompt. Expired prompts are the most common cause.
4. Make sure the computer's date and time are correct.
5. Try a phone hotspot to rule out the home router.
6. If it still fails, send IT a screenshot of the error.
