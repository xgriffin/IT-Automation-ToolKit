# Account locked out or password reset

**Applies to:** Microsoft 365 / Entra ID accounts

## Symptoms
- "Your account has been locked" message at sign in
- Too many wrong password attempts
- Forgot password

## Resolution
1. Wait 15 minutes. Smart lockout releases the account automatically.
2. Use self-service password reset at https://aka.ms/sspr if you're registered.
3. If that doesn't work, IT verifies your identity by phone or in person, then resets the password with "must change at next sign in" turned on.
4. Update the new password on your phone (Outlook and Teams apps) so old saved passwords don't lock the account again.

**Never** send a password by email or chat.
