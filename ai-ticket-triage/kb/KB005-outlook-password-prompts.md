# Outlook keeps asking for password / email not syncing

## Symptoms
- Repeated password prompts in Outlook
- Email not syncing or stuck "Disconnected"

## Resolution
1. Sign out of all Office apps (File > Office Account > Sign out), then sign back in.
2. Remove old saved credentials in Windows Credential Manager (entries starting with "MicrosoftOffice").
3. Confirm the account isn't locked and that MFA is registered.
4. Create a new Outlook profile from Control Panel > Mail if the issue continues.
