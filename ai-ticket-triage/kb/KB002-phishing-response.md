# Reporting a phishing or suspicious email

## Symptoms
- Unexpected email asking for gift cards, payments, wire transfers, or passwords
- Sender name looks right but the email address is wrong
- Urgent tone, links to sign in pages, unexpected attachments

## What the user should do
1. Don't click links, open attachments, or reply.
2. Use the **Report > Phishing** button in Outlook.
3. If you clicked a link or entered your password, tell IT **immediately**.

## What IT does
1. Reset the password and revoke sessions if credentials may be exposed.
2. Check sign in logs for unfamiliar locations.
3. Search for and purge the message from other mailboxes.
4. Block the sender or domain.
