# Printer paper jam or toner errors

## Symptoms
- Paper jam message
- "Toner low/empty" even after replacing the toner cartridge
- Print jobs stuck in the queue

## Resolution
1. Open all trays and doors and remove any jammed or torn paper.
2. Reseat the toner cartridge and remove all packing tape and seals.
3. Power cycle the printer.
4. Clear the print queue: restart the Print Spooler service on the print server.
5. If the toner error continues with a genuine cartridge, check the chip contacts and log a service call.
